# Code of conduct

Genoryn Krypton is built in public by people who may never meet. These rules keep that
workable.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Criticise code, designs and claims, not people.
- Be precise. Say what you observed, what you expected and how to reproduce it.
- Be honest. Do not misrepresent the project, its risks or other people's work.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you accuse.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, or trying to uncover the identity of a
  pseudonymous participant.
- Spam, advertising, price talk meant to move a market, and requests for anyone's recovery
  phrase, keys or passwords.
- Posting a vulnerability in public before it is fixed (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere someone
speaks for the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that breaks these rules and may block repeat
offenders. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment in question, or
open an issue if it can be discussed in public.

Nobody will ever ask for your recovery phrase. Anyone who does is trying to steal your coins.
