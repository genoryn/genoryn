What they will be

Private like GNR. Honest about their issuer.

Every transfer hidden

Coins will live in the same shielded note tree as GNR. A transfer proves in zero knowledge that everything balances; the chain sees only a proof, never the coin, the amount or the people.

Quantum-safe

Hash-based zk-STARK spends, post-quantum encryption and post-quantum issuer signatures. Nothing that guards a coin will depend on elliptic curves.

Declared, not hidden in code

No smart contracts. A coin will be a short public declaration: how many can exist, whether more can be created, whether holders can burn, whether transfers can be paused. Fixed forever and shown to every holder in plain words.

GNR at the core

Every fee will be paid in GNR, and coins will trade against GNR in one atomic transaction between two wallets: no exchange, no custody.

No look-alikes

Every name will be unique on chain, names of the network itself will be refused, and a list signed with the project's post-quantum key will mark issuers it has identified.

Supply anyone can audit

Transfers private, issues and burns public: the total of every coin will be known to everyone at every block, without trusting anyone.

Both sides move, or nothing does.

The chain sees one private transfer. An unfinished swap simply expires: nothing is ever locked.

Transparency

Every risk in plain words.

A coin will only be as good as whoever issues it. The network will guarantee that nobody can create, take or freeze coins in ways that were not declared; it cannot guarantee what a coin is worth. That is why every wallet will show, under every coin, what its issuer can do:

  • Supply: fixed for good, capped, or open to more issues.
  • Burning: whether holders can destroy their coins.
  • Pausing: whether the issuer can stop all transfers, and that such a coin shows when it moves.
  • Backing: what, if anything, the coin's value rests on.
  • Identity: whether the project has identified the issuer, which is never a promise of value.

What no issuer will ever be able to do: move your coins, see your balance, freeze one holder or change the terms after the fact.

FAQ

Questions about quantum coins.

When will quantum coins arrive?

In a future release. The network turns them on at a block height announced with that update, so every node and wallet can update first. Until then there is nothing to do.

Will coin transfers be private?

Yes, exactly like GNR: the coin, the amount, the sender and the recipient will be hidden by a zero-knowledge proof. Only the creation and destruction of coins will be public, so every supply can be audited.

Could an issuer freeze or take my coins?

No. No issuer will be able to take coins, see balances or freeze a single holder. Anything an issuer may do will be declared before the coin exists and shown to you before you accept it.

What will it cost?

Fees will be paid in GNR, like every transaction on Genoryn Krypton, and creating a coin will carry a fee paid to the miners.

Do I need to do anything now?

No. Keep your wallet up to date; the Coins tab turns on by itself when coins are activated.
