GNR market

People set the price of GNR themselves: with the offers they publish in the wallet and the swaps they complete. Every trade is an atomic swap between two wallets, so there is no exchange, no custody and no account.

Completed swaps

Each swap as the GNR blockchain proves it: the claim, its block and the secret that unlocked both sides. Amounts are private on chain; the ones shown were reported by the two wallets and count only once their claim is on chain.

How a swap works

  1. Get the wallet from the download page and keep it running.
  2. Open Swap: take an offer from the board, or publish your own.
  3. The seller's GNR is locked first. As soon as you take an offer, the seller's wallet locks the GNR on chain, payable only to you. Your wallet checks that lock (exactly the agreed amount, only you can claim it) and shows where to pay only after it has confirmed. A seller who does not have the GNR cannot lock it, so you never pay them.
  4. The wallets finish alone. The seller takes your bitcoin with its secret; your wallet reads that secret from the bitcoin transaction and claims the GNR. If anyone stops, timeouts refund both: the bitcoin after about 6 hours, the GNR after about 32. No swap lasts longer than a week.

How the market price is computed: the plain average of what people actually paid.

  • Only completed swaps count, each one the same, and only once its claim is on chain. Offers and swaps in progress are shown but never move the price: anyone can post an offer at any price.
  • The last 72 hours: the average of the swaps completed in that time, or of all recorded swaps while there are none that recent.
  • Typos are left out: with three or more swaps, a swap more than 4× away from their median does not count (amounts are reported by the wallets, since they are private on chain).

Your price is free: publish an offer at any price you like. The board only refuses offers for more than 1,000 BTC and offers without proof of work; offers whose maker is offline stay listed, greyed out, and cannot be taken. Prices are shown in bitcoin and in dollars at the current BTC price.
