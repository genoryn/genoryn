How to trade

  1. Get the wallet from the download page and keep it running.
  2. Open Swap: take an offer from the board, or publish your own at any price.
  3. The wallets do the rest. The buyer pays bitcoin into a contract, and the seller's GNR is locked only after that deposit confirms. Either both get paid, or both get their coins back.

How the price is computed: a volume-weighted median of everything people are doing on the board.

  • Swaps completed in the last 24 hours count three times. A swap only counts if its claim is on chain.
  • Swaps in progress count twice.
  • Open buy and sell offers count once, so the first offer already sets a price.

The price is shown in sat and in dollars at the current BTC price.

Cost to mine is shown for reference: the electricity the whole network spends per GNR at today's hashrate, assuming RTX 5090-class GPUs. With more miners it rises, because the same 7,200 GNR a day take more electricity. It is not a price.

Swaps on mainnet activate at block 3,300.
