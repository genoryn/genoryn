Why swap bitcoin for GNR

Move bitcoin's value into a quantum-resistant system.

Bitcoin's coins are guarded by elliptic-curve signatures (ECDSA and Schnorr). A large enough quantum computer running Shor's algorithm could break them and spend other people's coins. Nobody knows when such a machine will exist, but savings are kept for decades.

That is what this market is for. An atomic swap lets you move value from bitcoin into GNR, whose coins are guarded by hash-based zk-STARK proofs instead of elliptic curves, directly from your own wallet: no exchange holds your coins, no account, no identity. You can swap back to bitcoin the same way whenever you like.

  • For protectionValue you move into GNR no longer depends on elliptic-curve cryptography.
  • ProvenA sell offer is listed only after the board has checked that its GNR is there.
  • AtomicEither both sides are paid, or both keep their coins. Nobody can run off with half a trade.
  • Locked firstThe seller's GNR is locked on chain before the buyer sends any bitcoin.
  • Honest about limitsA swap exchanges bitcoin for GNR at the market price; the bitcoin itself stays on Bitcoin's chain with its new owner.

How a swap works

  1. Get the wallet from the download page and keep it running.
  2. Open Swap to buy or sell in one click, or Market to take an offer from the board or publish your own.
  3. The seller's GNR is locked first. As soon as you take an offer, the seller's wallet sees your bitcoin in your Bitcoin balance and locks the GNR on chain, payable only to you. Your wallet checks that lock (exactly the agreed amount, only you can claim it) and pays from the balance by itself only after it has confirmed. A seller who does not have the GNR cannot lock it, so you never pay them; and a buyer without bitcoin cannot take an offer, so a seller's GNR is never tied up for nothing.
  4. The wallets finish alone. The seller takes your bitcoin with its secret; your wallet reads that secret from the bitcoin transaction and claims the GNR. If anyone stops, timeouts refund both: the bitcoin after about 3 hours, the GNR after about 21. No swap lasts longer than a week.
