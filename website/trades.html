Where these figures come from

  • Every swap the offer board has recorded, once its GNR claim is on the GNR chain. A swap is an atomic swap between two wallets: no exchange took part, and nobody held anyone's coins.
  • Amounts are private on chain. The GNR and bitcoin shown were reported by the swap's own wallets. BTC ✓ means the board also found the swap's bitcoin on the Bitcoin chain, paid into the swap's contract and taken out with its secret.
  • The average price weighs each swap by the bitcoin it moved; the median is the price half of the swaps were below. Swaps whose two wallets reported different amounts are listed but left out of every figure.
  • The proof opens the GNR claim in the explorer: its block, and the secret that unlocked both sides of the swap.
  • The market price is computed separately, from the recent verified swaps: how the market price is computed.
