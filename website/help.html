Asked most

Before anything else

QuestionAnswer
I lost my password.The 24 words restore the wallet without it: add a wallet, choose Restore from recovery phrase and set a new password. Without the words and without the password, nobody can open the wallet, the project included.
I lost my 24 words.If you can still open the wallet, show them again under Security and write them down now. If you cannot, there is nothing anyone can do.
My payment says pending.It waits for the next block, usually within a minute or two. If it stays, check that the wallet is connected (the badge at the top says verified).
My swap says the GNR is locked and nothing happens.It is not lost. If the buyer never pays, the GNR comes back by itself at the block the swap shows, about 21 hours after the lock. Keep the wallet running then.
My mining rewards are not in my balance.Rewards build up and are paid as one note every 360 blocks, about six hours, and can be spent 100 blocks later. The Mining tab shows what is building up.
Is this version the newest?About → Check for updates. The newest release is always at the top of News.

What to put in your question

  • The version: the wallet's About tab, or kryptond --version.
  • Your system: Windows 11, Ubuntu 24.04, and for mining the graphics card.
  • What you did, what you expected and what you saw, with the exact words of any error.
  • Transaction and block ids are public and fine to post. Addresses are yours to share or not.

The wallet's Console can gather some of this for you: getversion, getnetworkinfo and getblockchaininfo show the version, how the wallet is connected and where the chain stands. None of them shows a key or a balance.

Tech support

Help someone else

Genoryn Krypton has no support department. The people who answer are people who use it. If you have run a miner for a week or set up a node, you already know things a newcomer is stuck on.

  • Answer questions. Open questions are listed here. Say what worked for you, and say when you are not sure.
  • Confirm bugs. "It happens for me too, on this system and this version" turns one report into a pattern.
  • Point to the page. If the answer is in the documentation, link it. If it is not there, that is a gap worth filling.
  • Never ask for, and never accept, anybody's recovery phrase, password or wallet file, and say so when you see someone else ask.
