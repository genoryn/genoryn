Buy on an exchange not yet

GNR is not listed anywhere yet. We are applying to exchanges that list privacy-focused proof-of-work coins. Listings will be announced only on genoryn.io and on GitHub; treat any other "GNR market" as a scam until then.

3

Swap BTC ⇄ GNR in the wallet live

Atomic swaps exchange Bitcoin and GNR directly between two people: either both sides receive their coins or both get their own back. There is no exchange, no custody and no account. The wallet's Swap tab has an offer board: publish an offer at your own price, or take someone else's. The seller's GNR is locked on chain for you first, and your wallet shows where to pay only once that lock has confirmed, so nobody can sell you GNR they do not have. Read how it works in docs/spec/atomic-swaps.md.

Stay safe

  • There is no presale, no token sale and no "early investor" round. Anyone offering one is not from the project.
  • Download the wallet only from genoryn.io or GitHub. Every release is signed with the project's post-quantum key, and the wallet checks that signature before it updates itself.
  • Never share your 24 words. Nobody from the project will ever ask for them.
