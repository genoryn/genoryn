Quantum tokens.

In a future release, anyone will be able to deploy a token on Genoryn Krypton: stablecoins, project tokens, tokens brought from other networks. Each will move like GNR, with every transfer a zero-knowledge proof, private and safe from quantum computers, and with everything its issuer may ever do declared in public before the first token exists.

What they will be

Private like GNR. Honest about their issuer.

Every transfer hidden

Tokens will live in the same shielded note tree as GNR. A transfer proves in zero knowledge that everything balances; the chain sees only a proof, never the token, the amount or the people.

Quantum-safe

Hash-based zk-STARK spends, post-quantum encryption and post-quantum issuer signatures. Nothing that guards a token will depend on elliptic curves.

Declared, not hidden in code

No smart contracts. A token will be a short public declaration: how many can exist, whether more can be created, whether holders can burn, whether transfers can be paused. Fixed forever and shown to every holder in plain words.

GNR at the core

Every fee will be paid in GNR, and tokens will trade against GNR in one atomic transaction between two wallets: no exchange, no custody.

No look-alikes

Every name will be unique on chain, names of the network itself will be refused, and a list signed with the project's post-quantum key will mark issuers it has identified.

Supply anyone can audit

Transfers private, issues and burns public: the total of every token will be known to everyone at every block, without trusting anyone.

How trading will work

Both sides move, or nothing does.

1

The buyer

prepares its GNR payment to the seller, without the proof that makes it valid.

2

The seller

checks that it receives the agreed GNR, adds the token for the buyer and proves its half.

3

The buyer

checks that it receives the agreed token, proves its half and sends the one transaction.

4

Done

The chain sees one private transfer. An unfinished swap simply expires: nothing is ever locked.

Transparency

Every risk in plain words.

A token will only be as good as whoever issues it. The network will guarantee that nobody can create, take or freeze tokens in ways that were not declared; it cannot guarantee what a token is worth. That is why every wallet will show, under every token, what its issuer can do:

  • Supply: fixed for good, capped, or open to more issues.
  • Burning: whether holders can destroy their tokens.
  • Pausing: whether the issuer can stop all transfers, and that such a token shows when it moves.
  • Backing: what, if anything, the token's value rests on.
  • Identity: whether the project has identified the issuer, which is never a promise of value.

What no issuer will ever be able to do: move your tokens, see your balance, freeze one holder or change the terms after the fact.

FAQ

Questions about quantum tokens.

When will quantum tokens arrive?

In a future release. The network turns them on at a block height announced with that update, so every node and wallet can update first. Until then there is nothing to do.

Will token transfers be private?

Yes, exactly like GNR: the token, the amount, the sender and the recipient will be hidden by a zero-knowledge proof. Only the creation and destruction of tokens will be public, so every supply can be audited.

Could an issuer freeze or take my tokens?

No. No issuer will be able to take tokens, see balances or freeze a single holder. Anything an issuer may do will be declared before the token exists and shown to you before you accept it.

What will it cost?

Fees will be paid in GNR, like every transaction on Genoryn Krypton, and creating a token will carry a fee paid to the miners.

Do I need to do anything now?

No. Keep your wallet up to date; the Tokens tab turns on by itself when tokens are activated.
