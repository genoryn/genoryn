Features

Money you can check for yourself.

Genoryn Krypton is one piece of open-source software in four programs: a wallet, a full node, a miner and a command-line wallet. Together they let you hold, send, mine and trade GNR without asking anyone's permission and without trusting anyone's word. These pages say what the software does, how, and where its limits are.

In depth

Six things it is built to do well

Validation

A full node checks every block and every proof against every rule, so no miner, exchange or website can make you accept GNR that breaks them. The light wallet checks the proof of work behind what it is told.

How validation protects you

Privacy

Sender, recipient and amount of every transfer are hidden by a zero-knowledge proof. See exactly what the chain shows, what a node can learn about you, and what stays public on purpose.

What is hidden, and from whom

Quantum security

Spending is a hash-based zk-STARK proof and payment data is encrypted with ML-KEM, NIST's post-quantum standard. Nothing that guards coins or privacy can be broken by Shor's algorithm.

What quantum computers break

Requirements

The light wallet runs on an ordinary computer and needs no chain download. A full node needs about 0.5 GB of disk today. Mining wants a graphics card, and works on a processor.

What your computer needs

User interface

A desktop wallet with a built-in miner, swap board and console; a command-line wallet; a node with a REST API, JSON-RPC and a WebSocket; SDKs for TypeScript, Rust and C.

Every way to use it

Network support

Every full node carries blocks and transactions to others over links encrypted against quantum computers, and makes the network harder to split, censor or lie to.

Also

What else it does

Mining that pays by work

Every block's reward goes into a pool that consensus holds, and every share a miner finds earns an equal slice of it. One percent of the hashrate earns about one percent of the rewards, alone, from the first hour.

Mining

Trading without an exchange

BTC and GNR change hands in atomic swaps between two wallets: either both sides are paid or neither is. A sell offer must prove its GNR to the board before it is listed.

Market

Proof of a payment

A payment proof shows one payment, its amount, memo and recipient, to whoever you give it to, and nothing else about your wallet. Anyone can check it on the explorer.

Payment proofs

Try it

The wallet is free, open source and needs no account. Your keys are made on your computer and never leave it.
