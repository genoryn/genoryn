# Internal security review, October 2026

Scope: `genoryn-core` (codec, PoW, difficulty, emission, shielded validation,
chain state, storage, fork choice, mempool), the node (`p2p.rs`, `node.rs`,
seeds, configuration), `genoryn-wallet-core`, the wallet CLI/GUI IPC, release and
update handling and the CI workflows. This is an internal review, not the
independent audit required by the mainnet gate.

No consensus rule changed. Every committed vector, including the real devnet
block 1, still validates, and a patched node synchronized the public devnet from
its seed and replayed its stored chain after restart.

## Findings and fixes

| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|---|----------|---------|------------|
| 1 | High | A block naming any stored block as parent forced a replay of that whole branch from genesis (RandomX and Halo2 for every block) before its own work was checked. | Branch header context and the block's RandomX work are verified first; unknown parents are refused without replay. |
| 2 | High | A full mempool, a duplicate, an anchor one block ahead or a clock-skewed timestamp gave honest relays 10 strikes per transaction, so they were disconnected and banned. | Policy rejections are typed and cost nothing; only invalid data is punished. |
| 3 | Medium | `GetBlocks` hashed every active block per locator under the global lock, unbounded in rate; each request could return a 2 MB block. | Indexed lookup; per-connection request limits. |
| 4 | Medium | The discovery cache kept its first 128 entries forever and accepted unsolicited `Peers`; one IP could hold every inbound slot. | Per-netgroup admission cap, solicited replies only, failing addresses forgotten, four inbound connections per IP. |
| 5 | Medium | Each new block cloned the entire in-memory chain including bodies and the state twice; difficulty and median collected all headers; startup re-verified all work, proofs and the full nullifier root per block. Cost grew quadratically with height. | In-place application, tail-only header context, stored-block replay without repeated work/proof/nullifier-root verification. |
| 6 | Medium | The mining template held the node lock while creating two Halo2 proofs, stalling peers for seconds per block. | Selection under the lock; proving outside it. |
| 7 | Low | A full mempool refused every newcomer regardless of fee. | Displaces strictly lower fee rates, only after the newcomer verifies. |
| 8 | Low | Mempool byte accounting re-encoded the whole pool for every admission. | Sizes are tracked. |
| 9 | Low | The proof cache was cleared wholesale at 1,024 entries. | FIFO eviction, 4,096 entries. |
| 10 | Low | Ban and dial-retry tables grew without bound. | Pruned and capped. |
| 11 | Low | Moving the local clock back over two minutes stopped the node from opening its own database. | The future-time rule applies at admission only. |
| 12 | Low | Public RPC shared one concurrency budget with the admin RPC. | Separate budgets. |
| 13 | Info | The published seed list could follow a redirect to plain HTTP. | HTTPS only. |

## Reviewed without defects

Canonical bounded decoding and trailing-byte rejection; integer PoW target
arithmetic; bounded LWMA retarget and time rules; integer emission and treasury
vesting; coinbase and treasury context, amount and receiver checks; nullifier
uniqueness within transactions, blocks and the mempool; signature hash binding of
network, expiry and bundle commitment; anchor window; wallet storage (BIP39,
Argon2id, XChaCha20-Poly1305, zeroization); constant-time admin token check and
loopback-only admin binding; stdin-only secret IPC; release-manifest path and
signature checks; pinned CI actions with read-only default permissions.

## Residual risks

These need a coordinated protocol upgrade or new sync protocol, so they were not
changed on the running devnet:

- Sync is block-by-block without headers-first download or minimum chain work;
  low-difficulty early heights make stored low-work side blocks cheap.
- The nullifier root hashes the entire nullifier set for every new block; an
  incremental commitment should replace it in the next protocol version.
- Devnet and testnet treasury keys are public by design. (At the time of this
  review mainnet was disabled; it launched on 10 October 2026 with a burned
  treasury and without an external audit.)
- The Orchard/Halo 2 integration and this codebase still require independent
  cryptography, consensus and network audits; the mainnet launch did not
  change that. See also the [network-privacy review](network-privacy-review-2026-10.md).
