# GENORYN 1.0.3

A wallet update for Mainnet 1.0. Consensus, the chain, the node and wallet files are
unchanged; update by running the new installer (wallets are kept).

## Wallet

- **Balances always show in full.** Every amount is the exact value with thousands grouped,
  for example `54,000.9999 GNR` or `7,000.99995 GNR`, and is never rounded or cut off: a
  figure wider than its space (a large balance in a narrow window or in the list of open
  wallets) shrinks to fit instead.
- The open wallet's balance card, with its sync state ("Fully synced at block … · verified
  by your own node"), stays at the top of the Overview.

## genoryn.io

- The "Nodes seen · 24h" estimate was removed from the home and status pages; the live
  strip shows the issued supply instead.
