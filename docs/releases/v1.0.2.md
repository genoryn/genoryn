# GENORYN 1.0.2

A wallet update for Mainnet 1.0. Consensus, the chain, the node and wallet files are
unchanged; update by running the new installer (wallets are kept).

## Wallet

- **Overview puts the open wallet first.** The balance of the wallet you are using is at the
  top. Other open wallets are listed beside it on wide windows and below it on narrow ones,
  with their balances, scan height and a Switch button; the combined total heads the list.
- **Uses wide windows.** Overview and Transactions grow with the window instead of leaving
  an empty strip on large screens, and still fit small ones.
- **Payment notices.** A short notice in the top-right corner, shown once and gone after a
  few seconds, tells you when a wallet received payments, when a payment you made is on its
  way, when it is confirmed and when the wallet got new block rewards, for example
  "Main Miner: 2 new block rewards (2000 GNR)". Old history is never announced again.
