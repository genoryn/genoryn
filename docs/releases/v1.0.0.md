# GENORYN 1.0.0 — Mainnet 1.0

GENORYN 1.0.0 launches **a new mainnet** with a new genesis block and chain identity, and a
wallet that works with or without a node of its own while validating every block itself.

> GENORYN has not been independently audited. Mine or hold only what you can afford to lose.

The earlier network (`genoryn-mainnet-v1`, genesis `c5fc87d9…d708`, releases 0.4–0.6) is
**retired**. 1.0 refuses its peers (different magic and port), its blocks (different genesis)
and its transactions (signatures commit to the genesis; a real retired-network transfer is
part of the test suite and fails verification). Its coins do not exist on 1.0, and restoring
an old recovery phrase does not carry any balance over. Data of the retired network stays in
its old directory and is never reused.

## Monetary policy: blocks 1–500 pay 1,000 GNR

| Height | Miner subsidy per block |
|---|---|
| 0 (genesis) | 0 — no premine, no allocation |
| **1 – 500** | **1,000 GNR**, the entire subsidy (500,000 GNR in total, ≈ 2.38 % of 21,000,000) |
| 501 – 2,102,900 | 4.825437595129 GNR, plus 1 GNR per block to the treasury (burned on mainnet) for 210,000 blocks |
| later | halves every 2,102,400 blocks; no tail emission |

The earlier network's 2 GNR bootstrap bonus is replaced entirely by the 500 early blocks, so
the two are never added together. Lifetime issuance is 20,999,999.999950064 GNR and never
exceeds the 21,000,000 GNR cap. **There is no founder-only minting privilege**: the reward goes
to whoever mines a valid block. **At launch the only known miner is run by the project
operator**, who may therefore receive most or all of the 500,000 early GNR. Mining early is not
a promise of profit or price. Details and risks:
[GENORYN Monetary Policy & Early Mining Incentives](../protocol/emission.md).

The initial difficulty is 150,000 (about 60 seconds of the launch miner's measured
~3,100 hashes per second), so the first blocks cannot be mined in moments; the LWMA retarget
follows the real hashrate from block 12 on.

## Wallet

- **Quick mode and Full mode.** Quick mode runs without a node: blocks come from remote nodes'
  public RPC only over Tor, every block is validated on your computer (RandomX, Halo 2 proofs,
  state roots) and kept in an encrypted verified chain, so later syncs download only new
  blocks. Full mode uses your own node. Switching keeps keys, scan progress, pending payments
  and mining history.
- **Fork choice by cumulative work.** Quick mode follows the valid chain with the most work
  computed locally; on equal work it keeps the chain it already follows. A higher chain with
  less work, a source with another genesis or invalid blocks, or a stale source is never
  followed. If no source answers, the last verified state is shown as such.
- **Durable outbox.** Payments are signed locally, their exact bytes and transaction ID are
  stored encrypted and synced to disk, and only then sent. Lost answers, Tor or network
  failures and restarts lead to the same bytes being sent again, never to a second spend. The
  node's transaction ID must match the local one. Coins stay reserved until the chain shows
  the payment confirmed, spent elsewhere or expired. Statuses: Pending, Submitted, Confirmed,
  Expired, Conflicted, Reorged.
- **Mining history.** Rewards are Just mined, Maturing, Mature or Replaced, with progress to the
  consensus unlock height. A reward replaced by a heavier chain stays in the history and is
  never counted; a connection error is never treated as a replacement.
- **Honest balances.** An unknown balance is never shown as 0; a balance from unreachable
  sources reads "last verified at block H".
- **Wallet birthday.** New wallets skip decrypting blocks from before their creation (with a
  safety margin); restored wallets scan their whole history unless given a restore height.
- **Tor switching.** Choosing Tor only, Hybrid or Direct applies the mode to the node the wallet
  owns through one serialized transition (a running miner pauses and resumes), with progress,
  Cancel and Retry; late results from before a change are discarded, and nothing ever falls
  back to a direct connection.
- **GENORYN Explorer** in the sidebar (opens only when clicked; in Tor-only mode it warns that
  the browser does not use GENORYN's Tor) and an **About** section with version, commit,
  chain ID and genesis.

## Node and miner

- Public RPC has a global rate limit in addition to its size, concurrency and timeout limits.
- The miner can place its shared RandomX dataset in 2 MiB pages
  (`GENORYN_RANDOMX_LARGE_PAGES=1` with `vm.nr_hugepages` reserved): about 16 % faster on the
  launch server. RandomX hashing and verification are unchanged.

## Verification

- Consensus suite with the new emission (heights 0, 1, 499, 500, 501, every halving, treasury
  boundaries, exact lifetime totals, property tests), a regenerated mined devnet vector with
  real Halo 2 and RandomX validation, and retired-network replay rejection.
- Quick-mode fork choice against real mined devnet chains (higher-but-lighter, equal work,
  heavier reorganization, incremental extension, tampered and unreachable sources).
- Real three-node devnet: early subsidy and maturity statuses, payments and outbox
  confirmation, a lost acknowledgement retried with the same bytes, Quick mode over a SOCKS
  stand-in, a real reorganization keeping the replaced reward, recovery and restarts.
- Quick mode against the two mainnet onion services over the public Tor network.
