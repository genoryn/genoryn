**Mainnet release. The software has not been independently audited: mine or hold only what you can afford to lose.**

GENORYN 0.5.0 gives the wallet a new design, adds **GENORYN Lite**, a second installer
without mining, and stops payments and username registrations that the balance cannot
cover before they start.

### Two installers

| | GENORYN | GENORYN Lite |
|---|---|---|
| Installer | `GENORYN-Setup-0.5.0.exe` | `GENORYN-Lite-Setup-0.5.0.exe` |
| Wallet: send, receive, usernames, payment proofs | ✓ | ✓ |
| Own full node that validates everything | ✓ | ✓ |
| CPU mining | ✓ | — |

GENORYN Lite has no miner program at all and no Mining page; everything else is the
same. Each edition updates only to itself, and both can be installed side by side
(they share your wallets in `%USERPROFILE%\.genoryn\mainnet`). Portable `.zip`
packages of both editions are attached for Windows and Linux.

### A new wallet design

- The typography of [genoryn.io](https://genoryn.io/): Inter for text, Unbounded for
  headings and balances, JetBrains Mono for labels.
- Larger inputs with room around the text, 15 px body text everywhere, pill-shaped main
  actions, rounded cards and the same monochrome palette, dark by default.
- Motion: a short launch animation, a ring loader for work in progress, and a soft fade
  between pages.
- A dark installer with GENORYN artwork, and no install-mode question: both editions
  install for your account only, without administrator rights.

### Payments and usernames need a matured balance

- **Review payment** and **Register username** are disabled while the matured balance
  cannot pay the amount plus the fee. The reason is shown in the card in exact numbers,
  for example "10.00001 GNR needed including the fee, 5 GNR spendable".
- The confirm dialogs check again, and a refused payment or registration is shown in red
  in its own card instead of only in the status bar.
- The wallet now spends its largest notes first, so a payment uses the 16-input limit
  only when it really has to. When even 16 notes are not enough, the error says how to
  combine them.
- **A second payment before the next block works.** Previously a payment, then a
  username registration (or another payment) before the next block could pick the same
  coins and be refused as a double spend. Nodes now publish the nullifiers waiting in
  their mempool (`GET /v1/mempool/nullifiers`, public information already relayed by
  every peer), and the wallet leaves coins that a pending transfer spends alone.

### Smaller fixes

- Enter in the password field unlocks, creates or restores the wallet.
- Each page keeps its own scroll position, so a page never opens scrolled halfway down.
- The miner reports its hashrate every 10 seconds while it searches, so live metrics
  appear immediately instead of only after a block.
- No dark line under the top bar in light mode.

### Mining shows what to expect

The Mining page shows how often this computer finds a block on average (difficulty ÷
your hashrate) and its share of the network. When more miners join, difficulty rises and
blocks arrive less often for everyone; the page now makes clear that mining is still
working while you wait.

### README and artwork

New screenshots of every page, a full tour of the wallet, and PNG artwork rendered with
the wallet's own fonts, which fixes the "Why it is safe" image on GitHub. The public
explorer at [genoryn.io](https://genoryn.io/) shows the live chain.

### Upgrading

Run `GENORYN-Setup-0.5.0.exe` (or the Lite installer) over your current installation;
wallets and chain data are kept. 0.5.0 is fully compatible with 0.4.x nodes.

GENORYN is not code-signed yet. If Windows 11's Smart App Control is on, the installer
explains how to turn it off. SmartScreen's "Windows protected your PC" prompt: choose
**More info → Run anyway**.

### Verify before running

Every installer and archive has a matching `.sha256` file; each package contains
`SHA256SUMS` and `release-manifest.json` with the exact source commit and edition.
Packages are **unsigned builds**.
