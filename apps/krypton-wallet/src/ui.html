Welcome

A quantum-resistant, private wallet. Your keys never leave this computer; balances are verified locally, no full node required.

Your recovery phrase

Write these 24 words with their numbers on paper, in order, and keep them offline. Anyone with them controls your funds. They will not be shown again.

384 bits of entropy: a 192-bit margin even against quantum computers. A built-in checksum catches any wrong word or number when you restore.

Unlock

Every wallet has its own password. Once unlocked, a wallet stays open for 24 hours, then locks itself and asks for its password again. The miner keeps running either way.

Security

      
Spend proofs: hash-based zk-STARKs (no elliptic curves). Note encryption: X25519 + ML-KEM-1024 (FIPS 203). Wallet file: Argon2id 256 MiB + XChaCha20-Poly1305.
