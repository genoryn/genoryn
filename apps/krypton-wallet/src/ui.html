Your Quick Swaps

Why swap bitcoin for GNR

Move value out of reach of quantum computers.

Bitcoin's coins are guarded by elliptic-curve signatures, which a large quantum computer could one day break. A swap moves that value into GNR, which is guarded by hash-based proofs instead, directly between two wallets: no exchange, no custody, no account. You can swap back to bitcoin the same way at any time.

  • ProvenA sell offer is listed only after the board has checked that its GNR is there.
  • Locked firstThe seller's GNR is locked on chain before the buyer pays.
  • AtomicBoth sides are paid, or both keep their coins.
  • AnonymousOffers carry one-time keys, never your address.
Swap directly with someone, using codes

Buy GNR with bitcoin

The seller locks the GNR for you first; you pay only once it is locked on chain. Either both of you get paid, or both get your own coins back.

Paste a swap code

Codes start with gks1: a request from someone who wants to buy your GNR, or the next code of a swap in progress.

How it works

  1. Request. The buyer creates a request code and sends it to the seller.
  2. Accept. The seller pastes it and sends back an accept code.
  3. Lock, then pay. The seller's wallet locks the GNR for the buyer at once. Only when that lock has confirmed with exactly the agreed amount does the buyer's wallet show where to pay; the buyer then sends the deposit code.
  4. Automatic. The seller takes the bitcoin with its secret, and the buyer's wallet claims the GNR with that same secret. If anyone stops, timeouts refund both: the bitcoin after about 3 hours, the GNR after about 21. No swap lasts longer than a week.

On the board all of this happens by itself. A buyer never pays a seller who does not have the GNR: without a confirmed lock there is nothing to pay. The buyer pays within 2 hours of the lock, or the swap ends.

How the offer board works

Every sell offer is proven (✓): its GNR sits in a note of its own, the board has checked a zero-knowledge proof of that, and it takes the offer down the moment that GNR moves. A buy offer with a ✓ has its bitcoin deposited: the board found it on the Bitcoin chain, and the buyer's wallet pays the swap by itself. Offers are anonymous: each has its own one-time post-quantum key, never your address. Taking an online offer (green dot) starts the swap within seconds, with no approval: the maker's wallet answers by itself. The two wallets pass the codes through the board, sealed so the board cannot read them. Market price: from the swaps completed in the last 72 hours whose bitcoin the board found on the Bitcoin chain. It is their volume-weighted median: half of that bitcoin changed hands below it, half above, and no single swap counts for more than a fifth. Offers and swaps in progress never move it, so offer at any price you like.

What an offer commits you to

A sell offer sets its GNR aside and proves it to the board; it is sold only to buyers whose bitcoin is already there, and the GNR comes back if a buyer never pays. A buy offer is paid from your Bitcoin balance into an address of this wallet; a seller's GNR is locked for you before your wallet pays, and unsold bitcoin comes back to the balance. Where these figures come from

Every completed swap the board has recorded, newest first. A swap counts once its GNR claim is on the GNR chain; the amounts are private on chain, so the ones shown were reported by the two wallets of the swap. BTC ✓ means the board also found the swap's bitcoin on the Bitcoin chain. The average price weighs each swap by the bitcoin it moved; the median is the price half of the swaps were below. Swaps whose two wallets disagree are left out of the figures. The proof link opens the GNR claim in the explorer.

Language

        
        
        
Changes at once: mining, sync and swaps keep running. Translations other than English are machine translations; corrections are welcome on GitHub. Your recovery phrase, addresses, swap codes and the console always stay as they are.

      

      
Display

        
        
Market price: set by the people who trade GNR on the offer board. It comes from completed swaps only: each one's claim is on the GNR chain and its bitcoin on the Bitcoin chain, and the price is where half of that bitcoin changed hands below and half above. Offers never move it, so you may offer at any price. Converted to dollars with the BTC price, which arrives through genoryn.io so no third party sees this wallet. Before the first completed swap, no value is shown.

Your wallet file

        
The file is encrypted with your password: without it, nobody can open it, on this computer or any other, not even with the file in hand. There is no backdoor and no recovery service.

        
Key from the passwordArgon2id · 256 MiB · 3 passes

        
EncryptionXChaCha20-Poly1305

        
Tamperingdetected, file refused

        
The only attack on a stolen file is guessing the password, and every guess costs a quarter of a gigabyte of memory and about a second. A strong password (the meter under a new password shows one) puts it out of reach of any computer, quantum ones included. Your 24 words restore the wallet without the file.
