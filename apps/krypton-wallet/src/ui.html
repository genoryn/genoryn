Your recovery phrase

Write these 24 words with their numbers on paper, in order, and keep them offline. Anyone with them controls your funds. They will not be shown again.

384 bits of entropy: a 192-bit margin even against quantum computers. A built-in checksum catches any wrong word or number when you restore.

Unlock

Every wallet has its own password. Once unlocked, a wallet stays open for 24 hours, then locks itself and asks for its password again. The miner keeps running either way.

Offers are anonymous: each has its own one-time post-quantum key, never your address. Taking an online offer (green dot) starts the swap within seconds, with no approval: the maker's wallet answers by itself, and the GNR of sell offers is reserved so it is always there. Your swaps

Keep this wallet open (running and unlocked) until your swaps are done. Only your wallet holds the keys that lock, take and refund your coins, so only it can do these steps; genoryn.io never has your keys, which is also why nobody can take your coins. You do not need to watch: closing the window keeps the wallet working in the background. If the wallet locks itself (after 24 hours), unlock it again: the swap waits, and the timelocks give hours of room.

Buy GNR with bitcoin

The seller locks the GNR for you first; you pay only once it is locked on chain. Either both of you get paid, or both get your own coins back.

Paste a swap code

Codes start with gks1: a request from someone who wants to buy your GNR, or the next code of a swap in progress.

How it works

  1. Request. The buyer creates a request code and sends it to the seller.
  2. Accept. The seller pastes it and sends back an accept code.
  3. Lock, then pay. The seller's wallet locks the GNR for the buyer at once. Only when that lock has confirmed with exactly the agreed amount does the buyer's wallet show where to pay; the buyer then sends the deposit code.
  4. Automatic. The seller takes the bitcoin with its secret, and the buyer's wallet claims the GNR with that same secret. If anyone stops, timeouts refund both: the bitcoin after about 6 hours, the GNR after about 32. No swap lasts longer than a week.

On the board all of this happens by itself. A buyer never pays a seller who does not have the GNR: without a confirmed lock there is nothing to pay. The buyer pays within 2 hours of the lock, or the swap ends.

Your wallet file

        
The file is encrypted with your password: without it, nobody can open it, on this computer or any other, not even with the file in hand. There is no backdoor and no recovery service.

        
Key from the passwordArgon2id · 256 MiB · 3 passes

        
EncryptionXChaCha20-Poly1305

        
Tamperingdetected, file refused

        
The only attack on a stolen file is guessing the password, and every guess costs a quarter of a gigabyte of memory and about a second. A strong password (the meter under a new password shows one) puts it out of reach of any computer, quantum ones included. Your 24 words restore the wallet without the file.
